Overview

The big idea

A people-centred political engagement and community opportunity platform — built on a simple physical card, QR technology, digital communication and grassroots community clusters.

People Connect Initiative establishes a continuous connection between citizens, candidates, communities and legitimate opportunities across Oyo State.

Its goal is to onboard the people — most importantly young and eligible voters between the ages of 18 and 45 — to the party platform and political candidates.

We aim to reach a million plus prospective voters across the state through wards, local governments, constituencies and senatorial districts by engaging PCI Connectors.

At the heart of the initiative is the PCI Card. In its strongest form the card becomes the physical gateway into a broader PCI ecosystem — connecting citizens, candidates, party structures, information, opportunities and community welfare.

  • A million plus prospective voters targeted across Oyo State.
  • Young and eligible voters aged 18 – 45 at the centre.
  • Reach built ward by ward through PCI Connectors.
